        How this works

        Every Monday a GitHub Action queries OpenAlex for eight astronautics research topics and enriches the results with Semantic Scholar influential-citation counts. It then commits a fresh data/dataset.json and redeploys the site. There's no server or database, and it costs nothing to run.

        • Citations: total times cited, per OpenAlex.
        • Momentum: citations received this year plus last year.
        • Influential: citations where the paper meaningfully shaped the citing work (Semantic Scholar).
        • FWCI: field-weighted citation impact; 1.0 is the world average for similar papers.

        Download and read counts aren't openly published by most aerospace publishers, so momentum and influential citations stand in for demand.
